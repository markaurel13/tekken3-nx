TMST 1.0.0 for [Tekken 3 Expanded](https://github.com/omarma/tekken3-expanded) brings Tekken 5 DR music in the Jukebox, True Ogre's transformation before Tekken 3's Grand Final, Tekken 2's stages on the GAME OPTION's STAGES row, the Jukebox's music in the Android APK, and the fixes since 0.3.0. It runs on Expanded 1.5.0 and 1.5.1. No game data is included: the setup makes everything from your own discs.

**Expanded 1.5.0 and later need TMST 0.2.0 or later.** 1.0.0 is recommended for everyone, and needed with Expanded 1.5.1.

## New

- **Tekken 5 DR music in the Jukebox:** the setup takes Tekken 5 Dark Resurrection's music from the ISO the Expanded's setup was given (none without it). Pieces turned off with `--disable` in a title list are not made. The music stays in the games' own ADPCM / XA, so it takes about a quarter of the room it took before.
- **True Ogre's intro (Tournament Mode):** before Tekken 3's Grand Final, Ogre gets up and transforms into True Ogre.
- **TEKKEN 2 on the STAGES row** ([#11](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/11)): GAME OPTION's STAGES row has a TEKKEN 2 setting, and RANDOM picks from Tekken 2's stages too. The fighters not in Tekken 2 have their own Tekken 2 stages in RANDOM. In the school outfit they get no Tekken 2 stage.
- **The Jukebox's list in a bigger, sharper font** ([#10](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/10)): the list, hints and dialog are drawn by the GPU in a bolder font, sharp next to the HD faces.
- **The Jukebox's music in the Android APK:** included by default. `--no-music` leaves it out. Since the music now stays in the games' own ADPCM / XA, it fits: the whole Jukebox music went from 2.82 GB to 0.84 GB (about 30%). Keeping the ADPCM / XA saved 1.67 GB, and the pieces turned off with `--disable` another 0.32 GB. The APK has about 840 MB of music, 718 MB of it compressed in the APK.
- **Expanded 1.5.1:** the stock fighters' HD faces show on Tournament Mode's screens (headers and art).

## Fixes

- **Tournament Mode's round lamp and title** ([#4](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/4)): the new round's lamp now blinks after a KO, and the TOURNAMENT MODE title is drawn behind the fighters.
- **BGM SELECT on the Tekken 2 stages** ([#9](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/9)): the Tekken 2 stages and Tournament Mode follow RANDOM, ARCADE and SILENT. Their music plays with the Jukebox off too.
- **Hong Kong Rooftop** ([#12](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/12)): the floor's running lights and its orange ↔ teal fade work as in Tekken 2 (Classic and Remake).
- **Tournament Mode costumes** ([#14](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/14)): the costume and model you pick (R1: the third costume) stay for every match. The MOVESET card is the same as the Expanded's, and the menu row and the card are right after a RESET. Doctor B. and Tiger Jackson have their full names on the bracket (it said DR. B and TIGER), and no Team Battle mark shows before a boss's second form.
- **Music between rounds** ([#15](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons/issues/15)): after Mokujin's style loading, the fight's piece goes on, and the character select music no longer plays.
- **Tournament Mode's MOVESET card:** it offers every moveset (Jin's TEKKEN 5 DR one was missing).
- **Expanded 1.5.1:** the Jukebox's and Tournament Mode's pictures are no longer covered by the stock fighters' HD tiles. With player 2 in Practice, the STAGE line works, and every Grand Final boss intro plays. After a boss intro, the attract mode plays its TTT Embu again.

## Downloads

From this release on, each platform has its own ZIP below: `TMST-1.0.0-Windows.zip`, `TMST-1.0.0-macOS.zip`,
`TMST-1.0.0-Linux.zip`. Each holds one folder, `tmst`, ready to go into the Expanded's `addons` folder: no renaming.
The Android APK is built from the Windows or the macOS one.

## Updating from 0.3.0

1. **Close the game.**
2. **Replace the add-on:**
   - with git: `git -C "<the Expanded's folder>/addons/tmst" pull`;
   - from the ZIP: in your add-on's folder, delete everything **except the `workspace` folder** (the music and stages
     the setup made; without it they are made again, which takes a few minutes), then extract your platform's ZIP into
     the Expanded's `addons` folder. If your old add-on folder is not called `tmst` (e.g. `t3-expanded-addons-0.3.0`),
     move its `workspace` folder into the new `addons/tmst` and delete the old folder. `addons/tmst/addon.cmake` must
     exist.
3. **Start the Expanded's setup and click Update & play.** It sees the new version, runs the add-on's setup and builds
   the game again. Only what 1.0.0 changed is made again: the Tekken 1–2 music, the Tekken 2 stages, the Remake's
   stages and the TTT console music, in the games' own ADPCM / XA. The Tekken 5 DR music is new, and is made if the
   Expanded's setup has the Dark Resurrection ISO. **1.4.1 and earlier:** run `addons/tmst/Setup TMST.cmd` (macOS:
   `.command`, Linux: `.sh`) and click **Install & play**.
4. **Start the game.** Your mod settings, Jukebox choices and tournament progress stay.

If the TMST group is missing in the Mods tab afterwards, `.setup/setup.log` tells why; run `Setup TMST` once more.

## Fresh install

Extract your platform's ZIP into the Expanded's `addons` folder, then follow the
[README's installation](https://github.com/ttanko96/t3-expanded-addons#installation) for your platform.
